# Diamond Group company environments

The JR PHEEF foundation now has a modular company-world presentation layer for DGBO and FAMILY.

## Experience
- Each company opens as a contained, independently scrollable website-like environment inside the JR PHEEF workspace.
- Each environment has its own identity, hero image, sticky section navigation, service/content sections, relationship information and return navigation.
- Mobile layouts collapse content cards and preserve internal scrolling.
- The company pages use accessible button navigation and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## DGBO
The DGBO environment explains the group umbrella role, intended relationship with JR PHEEF and FAMILY, opportunity research, venture development, partnerships and governance. Company relationships are described as planned until legal and operational records are confirmed.

## FAMILY
The FAMILY environment provides financial education, planning and community information. It explicitly states that deposits, custody, lending, transfers and investment transactions are not active. Any regulated services require appropriately authorised providers and approvals.

## Images and advertising
Images use the trusted Unsplash image host, added to the server Content Security Policy. The content feed does not fabricate company news or paid advertisements. Sponsored campaigns must come from a reviewed, approved and separately verified advertising workflow before they appear as ads. The company pages currently show a neutral publication-feed placeholder instead of fake live activity.

## Not included
This change does not create new company registrations, financial products, ad billing, ad serving, third-party integrations or standalone public domains. It adds the in-app company environments and presentation layer; backend content management and ad-serving integrations remain separate work.
