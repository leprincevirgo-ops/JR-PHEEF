DIAMOND GLOBAL BUSINESS OPERATORS

Build beyond borders.

DGBO is the parent business-building organisation. Its role is to evaluate selected opportunities, develop viable ventures and operate approved businesses.

01

Opportunity review & venture research

Receive selected opportunity research from JR PHEEF, validate its market potential and decide whether DGBO should develop it.

02

Venture development

Structure a business concept, operating model, milestones and resource requirements.

03

Business connections

Prepare partnership enquiries and connect with organisations through reviewed pathways.

04

Company operations

Plan roles, workflows, records and governance for businesses in the ecosystem.

DGBO service discovery and planning are available here. Legal registration, investment handling, funding decisions and regulated services are not represented as active unless separately verified and authorised.

FAMILY · FINANCIAL ACCESS TO MONEY FOR INVESTMENTS & LOVING YOURSELF

Build your financial wellbeing together.

FAMILY has its own environment for financial education, personal and family goals, savings planning and access preparation.

01

Financial education

Learn about budgeting, saving, risk, responsible borrowing and investment basics.

02

Savings goals

Prepare personal savings targets and practical steps. This is planning, not custody of money.

03

Family planning

Organise shared priorities, education plans, emergency preparation and family milestones.

04

Authorised financial providers

Future account, payment, lending and investment services require licensed providers and approved integrations.

NOT ACTIVE · PARTNER DEPENDENT

FAMILY is not currently a bank, wallet, deposit-taking service or investment provider. No deposits, lending, payments, transfers or investment transactions are enabled in this workspace.
