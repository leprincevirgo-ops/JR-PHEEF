PLATFORM REGISTRATION

Joining JR PHEEF

  1. Create an account using your full name, email, phone number, birth year and password.
  2. Confirm your email address before signing in.
  3. Complete any additional identity verification requested when that service is activated.
  4. Review the terms, privacy notice and service-specific disclosures before using a service.

A JR PHEEF account is a platform account. It does not automatically register a company, make someone a DGBO partner, or enrol them in a FAMILY financial product.

OFFICIAL ORGANISATION

Diamond Global Business Operators

Diamond Global Business Operators (DGBO) is the parent business-building organisation. JR PHEEF is a connected platform within the DGBO ecosystem. FAMILY is a distinct financial-access and personal-growth service arm.

Before public commercial activation, this area must display the verified legal entity name, company registration number, registered office, tax details, relevant licences, privacy contact and service-provider responsibilities.

Those official legal identifiers have not yet been verified for publication in this staging build, so none are invented here. Payments, deposits, lending and investment transactions remain disabled.
