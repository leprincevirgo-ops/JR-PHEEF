04 · COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know

Is JR PHEEF a website, an application or a company?

JR PHEEF is the digital platform and product experience. It can be accessed through its website and installable web-app experience. The legal company that owns or operates it is a separate matter and must be described using verified registration details.

Is everything in the ecosystem available now?

No. This is a foundation staging experience. Some interfaces and workflows are implemented, some are partial, and some depend on external integrations or future approvals. JR should identify the actual status rather than promise unavailable functions.

Does joining mean I have invested or become a partner?

No. Creating a JR PHEEF account does not create an investment, partnership, employment relationship or company ownership.

Can I use JR for free?

Browsing and account access in this staging build do not activate a payment flow. Any future paid service must show its current price and terms before the user commits. Do not rely on old or proposed prices as active charges.

How is my information protected?

JR PHEEF is designed around account verification, access controls and private user data. Do not share passwords or one-time codes. Review the current privacy notice and use only services whose security and privacy controls have been verified.
