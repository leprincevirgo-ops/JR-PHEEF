Future Simulator · Business scenario lab

Explore how assumptions change a monthly business result. This is a simple illustrative model, not a forecast, financial advice, or a promise of earnings.

How JR PHEEF should reason

  1. Past: examine relevant history and previous outcomes.
  2. Present: identify current facts, constraints, and missing information.
  3. Possible futures: compare scenarios under visible assumptions.
  4. Choices: show trade-offs, risks, and alternatives.
  5. Action: choose a safe, measurable next step.
  6. Learning: compare actual outcomes with assumptions and improve the next decision.

Important claims should eventually show sources, dates, assumptions, and uncertainty. Private personal history must remain under user control.

Lab principles

1 · Ask

Define the real question and what a useful result would look like.

2 · Model

Identify evidence, assumptions, risks, resources and alternative explanations.

3 · Test safely

Use the smallest ethical, legal and affordable experiment that can teach something useful.

4 · Learn

Compare results with expectations, record limitations and improve the next test.
